PORT APP ICONS

These app marks identify the applications supported by Living Code. They are
not Living Code branding, and no endorsement or trademark rights are implied.
The monochrome adaptations are bundled locally in src/lib/port-app-icons.mjs
and src/lib/expanded-port-icons.mjs. Normal website builds do not download
artwork or run vectorization tools. Expanded-catalog upstream license texts
are retained in /port-icons/LICENSES.txt.
